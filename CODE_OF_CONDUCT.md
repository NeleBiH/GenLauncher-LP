# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GenLauncher-LP to be a friendly place for everyone who plays, mods or
contributes, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient — many people here are not native English speakers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/NeleBiH/GenLauncher-LP/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@NeleBiH](https://github.com/NeleBiH) on GitHub. All reports
are reviewed and kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, issues and pull requests, and may ban contributors who break this
code of conduct.
